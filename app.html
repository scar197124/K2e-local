What K2E can access
Only information you explicitly permit.
  • Manual mode: only the household and device information you enter into K2E.
  • Smart Insights: future compatible connections may read approved device status or usage data.
  • Controls: kept separate and never enabled simply because Insights are connected.
  • Disconnecting should return K2E to manual estimates without deleting your household device list.
